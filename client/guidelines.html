COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Keep the wall safe for everyone

These guidelines help maintain anonymity, respect, and safety. Posts that break these rules may be removed.

Do

  • Share your experience without naming people or places.
  • Offer encouragement and gentle reactions.
  • Use a secret code you can remember.
  • Pause before posting if emotions feel intense.

Do not

  • Post threats, harassment, or hate.
  • Reveal identifying details or private information.
  • Encourage self-harm or unsafe behavior.
  • Spam, advertise, or promote products.

Moderation flow

  • Posts are reviewed when they are reported.
  • Content that violates safety rules is removed.
  • Repeat violations may lead to account restriction.
  • Emergencies are escalated to campus safety protocols.
