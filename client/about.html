ABOUT THE WALL

Why this space exists

The confession wall lets students share truths they cannot say out loud. It is anonymous by design, focused on empathy, and built to help campus communities listen without judgment.

Our purpose

Make it easier to speak openly, connect with others, and feel less alone during intense seasons of campus life.

How anonymity works

Posts never display names, student IDs, or locations. Secret codes only let you edit or delete your own confession.

What happens after you post

Confessions appear on the wall instantly. Other students can react with support and encouragement.

Community values

  • Lead with empathy, even when you disagree.
  • Protect anonymity. Do not share identifying details.
  • Keep it safe: no harassment, threats, or hate.
  • Encourage support, not gossip.
