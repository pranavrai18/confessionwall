FAQ

Answers to common questions

Confessions are anonymous and designed for a supportive campus community. Here are the most asked questions.

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. Posts do not display names or profiles. Only your secret code lets you edit or delete what you wrote.

Can I edit or delete my confession?

Yes. Use the secret code you created when posting to update or remove your confession.

How are reactions used?

Reactions are simple, anonymous signals of support. They are not a voting system.

What if I see something unsafe?

Use the contact page to report it. Moderators review reports and take action.
